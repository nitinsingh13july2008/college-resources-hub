1 MARK

Effective Learning Skills

Q1. What is learning?

Learning is the process of gaining knowledge, skills and understanding through study or experience.

Q2. What is active learning?

Active learning means participating in the learning process through discussion, practice and activities.

Q3. What is time management?

Time management is the process of planning and using time effectively.

2 MARKS

Effective Learning Skills

Q4. Write two benefits of effective learning skills.

1. They improve understanding and memory.

2. They help students manage time and perform better in examinations.

Q5. What is the importance of note-making?

Note-making helps us record important information in a short and organized form. It makes revision easier.

6 MARKS

Effective Learning Skills

Q6. Explain different effective learning skills.

Effective learning skills help students understand and remember information properly.

1. Time Management: Planning study time according to priority.

2. Note-Making: Writing important points in a short form.

3. Active Listening: Paying attention during lectures.

4. Reading Skills: Reading carefully to understand information.

5. Practice: Revising and solving questions regularly.

6. Self-Assessment: Checking our own learning progress.

These skills improve academic performance and confidence.

1 MARK

Listening Comprehension

Q7. What is listening?

Listening is the process of receiving and understanding spoken messages.

Q8. What is active listening?

Active listening means listening carefully with full attention and understanding.

Q9. What is comprehension?

Comprehension means understanding the meaning of something heard or read.

2 MARKS

Listening Comprehension

Q10. Write two qualities of a good listener.

1. A good listener pays attention to the speaker.

2. A good listener does not interrupt and understands the message carefully.

Q11. Why is listening important?

Listening improves communication, understanding and learning. It also helps avoid misunderstandings.

6 MARKS

Listening Comprehension

Q12. Explain the importance of listening skills.

Listening is an important communication skill. It helps us understand spoken information.

1. Better Understanding: It helps us understand the speaker's message.

2. Better Communication: It improves interaction with others.

3. Learning: Students learn through lectures and discussions.

4. Relationships: It builds trust and respect.

5. Fewer Mistakes: Careful listening reduces misunderstandings.

6. Problem Solving: It helps us understand problems before responding.

1 MARK

Newspaper Reading

Q13. What is a newspaper?

A newspaper is a printed or digital publication that provides news and information.

Q14. What is a headline?

A headline is the title of a news article that tells its main topic.

Q15. What is an editorial?

An editorial is an article that expresses an opinion on an important issue.

2 MARKS

Newspaper Reading

Q16. Write two benefits of reading newspapers.

1. It increases general knowledge and awareness.

2. It improves vocabulary and reading skills.

Q17. What is the difference between news and an editorial?

News reports facts about events, while an editorial presents opinions and views about an issue.

6 MARKS

Newspaper Reading

Q18. Explain the importance of newspaper reading.

Newspaper reading is a useful habit for students and other people.

1. General Knowledge: It provides information about the world.

2. Vocabulary: It introduces new words.

3. Language Skills: It improves reading and writing.

4. Current Affairs: It keeps us updated about recent events.

5. Critical Thinking: It helps us understand different viewpoints.

6. Awareness: It increases knowledge about society and the country.

Parts of Speech

Q19. What is a noun?

A noun is the name of a person, place, animal or thing.

Example: Ram, Delhi, Book.

Q20. What is a pronoun?

A pronoun is a word used instead of a noun.

Example: He, She, They.

Q21. What is a verb?

A verb is a word that expresses an action or state.

Example: Run, Eat, Is.

1. Noun

2. Pronoun

3. Verb

4. Adjective

5. Adverb

6. Preposition

7. Conjunction

8. Interjection

6 MARKS

Parts of Speech

Q23. Explain the eight parts of speech with examples.

1. Noun: Name of a person, place or thing. Example: Boy.

2. Pronoun: Used instead of a noun. Example: He.

3. Verb: Shows action or state. Example: Play.

4. Adjective: Describes a noun. Example: Beautiful.

5. Adverb: Describes a verb or adjective. Example: Slowly.

6. Preposition: Shows relation. Example: In, On, At.

7. Conjunction: Joins words or sentences. Example: And, But.

8. Interjection: Expresses sudden feeling. Example: Wow!

1 MARK

Sentence Structure

Q24. What is a sentence?

A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought.

Q25. What is a subject?

The subject is the person or thing about which something is said.

Q26. What is a predicate?

The predicate tells something about the subject.

2 MARKS

Sentence Structure

Q27. What are the main parts of a sentence?

The two main parts of a sentence are:

1. Subject

2. Predicate

Example: Ram plays cricket.

Ram = Subject; plays cricket = Predicate.

6 MARKS

Sentence Structure

Q28. Explain the types of sentences with examples.

1. Assertive Sentence: Makes a statement. Example: I am a student.

2. Interrogative Sentence: Asks a question. Example: Are you ready?

3. Imperative Sentence: Gives a command or request. Example: Please sit down.

4. Exclamatory Sentence: Expresses strong feelings. Example: What a beautiful day!

5. Optative Sentence: Expresses a wish or prayer. Example: May you succeed!

VERB

Verb and Its Types

Q29. What are the main types of verbs?

1. Main Verb: Shows the main action. Example: She writes.

2. Helping Verb: Helps the main verb. Example: is, am, are.

3. Transitive Verb: Needs an object. Example: He eats an apple.

4. Intransitive Verb: Does not need an object. Example: Birds fly.
