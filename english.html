4. Parts of Speech

Parts of speech are categories of words based on their function in a sentence.

  • Noun – Name of a person, place or thing
  • Pronoun – Used instead of a noun
  • Verb – Shows action or state
  • Adjective – Describes a noun
  • Adverb – Describes a verb or adjective
  • Preposition – Shows relationship
  • Conjunction – Joins words or sentences
  • Interjection – Expresses emotion
✍️
