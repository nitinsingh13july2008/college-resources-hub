ENGLISH NOTES

English Language and Communication

Learn • Understand • Practise • Improve

1. Effective Learning Skills

Learning skills help students understand and remember information effectively.

  • Set clear learning goals.
  • Prepare a daily study timetable.
  • Make short and meaningful notes.
  • Revise topics regularly.
  • Avoid distractions while studying.
Example: Study for 30 minutes and revise for 5 minutes.

2. Listening Comprehension

Listening comprehension is the ability to understand spoken language and respond correctly.

  • Listen carefully to the speaker.
  • Identify the main idea.
  • Notice important facts and details.
  • Understand the speaker's purpose.
  • Answer questions based on what you hear.
Example: Listen to a short news report and write its main message.

3. Newspaper Reading

Newspaper reading improves vocabulary, reading skills and general awareness.

  • Read headlines to understand the topic.
  • Read articles carefully.
  • Find the main idea and supporting details.
  • Learn unfamiliar words.
  • Write a short summary.
Tip: Read one English news article daily.

4. Parts of Speech

Parts of speech classify words according to their function in a sentence.

  • Noun – name of a person, place or thing.
  • Pronoun – replaces a noun.
  • Verb – shows action or state.
  • Adjective – describes a noun.
  • Adverb – modifies a verb or adjective.
  • Preposition – shows relationship.
  • Conjunction – joins words or sentences.
  • Interjection – expresses sudden feelings.
Example: Wow! She writes very neatly.

5. Sentence Structure

A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought.

  • Subject – tells who or what the sentence is about.
  • Verb – tells the action or state.
  • Object – receives the action.
  • Sentences can be statements, questions, commands or exclamations.
Example: Ram (Subject) + plays (Verb) + cricket (Object).

6. Verb

A verb expresses an action, occurrence or state of being.

  • Main verbs: read, write, play.
  • Helping verbs: is, am, are, have, can.
  • Regular verbs: work → worked.
  • Irregular verbs: go → went.
  • Use verbs according to the subject and tense.
Example: She is reading a book.

7. Additional Grammar Practice

  • Tenses – present, past and future.
  • Articles – a, an and the.
  • Prepositions – in, on, at, under.
  • Subject-Verb Agreement – matching subject and verb.
  • Vocabulary – learning new words and meanings.
Example: He goes to college every day.
