TOPIC 01

📖 Effective Learning Skills

Q1. What are Effective Learning Skills?

Answer:
Effective learning skills are techniques and habits that help students understand, remember and use information effectively.

Q2. Explain the importance of Effective Learning Skills.

Answer:
Effective learning skills help students improve concentration, manage time, understand concepts, remember information and perform better in examinations.
TOPIC 01

Effective Learning Skills

Q3. Write five techniques for effective learning.

Answer:
  1. Time Management
  2. Note Making
  3. Regular Revision
  4. Practice
  5. Self-Assessment
TOPIC 02

🎧 Listening Comprehension

Q4. Define Listening Comprehension.

Answer:
Listening comprehension is the ability to listen carefully, understand spoken information and respond correctly.

Q5. What are the steps involved in Listening Comprehension?

Answer:
  1. Listen carefully.
  2. Understand the main idea.
  3. Identify important details.
  4. Remember key information.
  5. Answer the questions.
TOPIC 02

Listening Comprehension

Q6. Write the importance of listening skills.

Answer:
Listening skills improve communication, concentration, vocabulary and understanding of spoken English.
TOPIC 03

📰 Newspaper Reading

Q7. What is Newspaper Reading?

Answer:
Newspaper reading is the habit of reading news, articles, headlines and other information published in newspapers.

Q8. Explain the benefits of Newspaper Reading.

Answer:
  • Improves vocabulary.
  • Improves reading skills.
  • Increases general knowledge.
  • Improves grammar.
  • Provides information about current events.
TOPIC 03

Newspaper Reading

Q9. How can a student improve English through newspapers?

Answer:
A student can learn new words, understand sentence structures, read articles regularly and note down difficult vocabulary. This improves English language skills.

TOPIC 04 · PRACTICE

Identify Parts of Speech

Q12. Identify the Parts of Speech in the sentence:

Nitin plays cricket.
Answer:

Nitin → Noun
plays → Verb
cricket → Noun

Q13. Identify the Parts of Speech:

She is a beautiful girl.
Answer:

She → Pronoun
is → Verb
a → Article
beautiful → Adjective
girl → Noun

TOPIC 06

🔵 Verb

Q18. Define Verb with examples.

Answer:
A verb is a word that shows an action or state of being.
Examples: Run, Eat, Write, Play, Read.

Q19. What is a Main Verb?

Answer:
A main verb shows the main action in a sentence.
Example: She plays cricket.
TOPIC 06

Verb

Q20. What is a Helping Verb?

Answer:
A helping verb helps the main verb in a sentence.
Examples: is, am, are, was, were, has, have, will.

Q21. Differentiate between Transitive and Intransitive Verbs.

Transitive Verb:
It requires an object.
Example: He reads a book.

Intransitive Verb:
It does not require an object.
Example: The baby sleeps.
ADDITIONAL GRAMMAR

📌 Additional Practice

Q22. What are Tenses?

Answer:
Tense shows the time of an action. The three main tenses are Present, Past and Future.

Q23. What are Articles?

Answer:
Articles are words used before nouns.
The three articles are: A, An and The.
ADDITIONAL GRAMMAR

Prepositions

Q24. What are Prepositions?

Answer:
Prepositions show the relationship between words.
Examples: in, on, at, under, behind.
